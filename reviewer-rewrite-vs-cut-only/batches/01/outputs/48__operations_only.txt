Decisions
- The City Council approved the ordinance establishing a 12-month moratorium on the issuance of building permits for new residential standalone construction along commercial and transportation corridors in West Long Beach.
- The ordinance was declared urgent and will take effect immediately.
- The matter will be referred to the Planning Commission and planning staff to initiate a study of potential amendments to existing zoning and building regulations.

Action Items
- Planning Commission and planning staff will conduct a study to align zoning and building regulations with the city's more recent land use and urban design elements of the general plan.
